How To Play
Official Rules and Field Notes
OBJECTIVE

Identify where each live camera feed was captured by placing your best guess on the world map.

ROUND FLOW

Each round starts with a camera image. Study clues in signs, road markings, weather, architecture, terrain, and traffic behavior. Place your marker, then submit before time expires.

CAMERA REALITY

Not all cameras are created equal. Some update every few seconds. Some barely refresh during a full round. Some have glare, rain, spider webs, or a surprise bird collision. That randomness is part of the game and part of the challenge.

SCORING

Every round is worth up to 5,000 points based on distance from the real location. A default match is 5 rounds for a maximum total of 25,000 points.

CREDITS AND HINTS

Credits can help recover from a bad camera by buying hints, but spend them wisely. You do not know what quality of feed is coming next round, so resource management matters.
